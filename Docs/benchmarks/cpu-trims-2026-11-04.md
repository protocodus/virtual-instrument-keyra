# CPU: faded partials and the felt's update (0.4.16)

Two changes to `Source/DSP/KeyraEngine.cpp`, each measured on the eight
factory demos against the parent commit (`feat(tools): train, cross-validate
and backtest the keyboard network in one command`):

- **The felt's update.** While a damper holds a partial that both of its
  shares still hold, each chunk took three `std::pow` calls and raised every
  lane's rotation to the 32nd power by repeated squaring. The two shares'
  per-chunk factors now keep the base they were raised from and are reused
  while it holds still, which it does once the damper has landed, and a
  chunk's rotation is the partial's undamped per-chunk rotation, computed
  when its tuning changes, times the chunk's damping. The 24-bit demos are
  identical but for a few one-step differences in four of them.
- **Faded partials.** A partial was dropped once its power, in the output's
  units, fell under 1e-15, 150 dB under a full-scale peak; it is now dropped
  under 1e-12, 120 dB under.

## What the floor changes

Each 24-bit demo rendered at each floor, against the same render at 1e-15:
the largest change in any 100 ms window, and in any third-octave band of one
(25 Hz to 20 kHz, Hann).

| Floor | Loudest window of the change | Loudest band of the change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1e-14 | −124.1 to −138.1 dBFS | not measured |
| 1e-13 | −121.6 to −128.4 dBFS | −123.1 to −130.5 dBFS |
| 1e-12 | −112.0 to −119.0 dBFS | −114.2 to −119.7 dBFS |
| 1e-11 | −102.2 to −110.1 dBFS | not measured |

At 1e-12, in every band where the music itself reaches −90 dBFS, the change
stays 27 dB or more under it. The change grows 10 dB per decade of floor, as
the power it removes does. At 1e-12 it stays 20 dB under the smallest step
of 16-bit audio (−90 dBFS).
