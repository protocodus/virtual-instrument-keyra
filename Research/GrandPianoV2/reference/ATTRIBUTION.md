# Acoustic piano reference recordings

**Salamander Grand Piano V3 — Alexander Holm**, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Source distribution: [sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano), pinned at commit `3382bf9496bba2486f5ab0de55a264d1dfc38404`. [Original release](https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3). SFZ remapping by kinwie; the retuned variant by Markus Fiedler is not applied here.

These are recordings of a Yamaha C5 acoustic grand, captured with two AKG C414 microphones in AB configuration approximately 12 cm above the strings. Upstream describes 48 kHz/24-bit audio, 16 velocity layers and minor-third sampling. [Upstream description](https://sfzinstruments.github.io/pianos/salamander/).

The 24 downloaded FLAC files remain unchanged. WAV copies are lossless PCM24 decodes, without trimming, normalization, EQ or retuning. The upstream license and metadata are preserved in `upstream/`; hashes, URLs, velocity-bin mappings and onset observations are in `manifest.json`.

Velocity-bin midpoints are benchmark inputs, not measured hammer speeds. Recording pedal state, room details, editing history and exact release times are undocumented. These isolated sustain samples support tone/decay comparisons, but not a calibrated pedal benchmark. No endorsement by the recording author or instrument maker is implied.
